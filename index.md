# Troubleshooting OS

Welcome to the Troubleshooting OS documentation site.

This subsystem captures investigative workflows, root‑cause analyses (RCA), diagnostic case studies, and incident logs across LuckyAISystems.  
Each artifact documents how failures were observed, analyzed, and resolved — strengthening long‑term reliability and engineering reasoning.

---

## What’s Inside

### **Artifacts**
Structured troubleshooting write‑ups, including:
- Evidence collected  
- Hypotheses  
- Verification steps  
- Root cause  
- Resolution  
- Prevention patterns  

### **Incident Logs**
Chronological records of troubleshooting sessions and engineering investigations.

### **Templates**
Reusable RCA and diagnostic templates for future investigations.
